# qh-robots-s0

Build a simple multi-page website for a fictional small brand called "Qinghe Atelier".

Include: home, about, products, and contact pages.

The contact page should have a form with name, email, and message, and show a success message after submission.

Use placeholder text and placeholder images only. Do not use real brand names, logos, addresses, or phone numbers.

Do not integrate real payments, email, SMS, or analytics. Do not collect real personal information.
